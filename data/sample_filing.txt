The Company's business is subject to a number of risks, including but not

   client trust.

3. Interest rate sensitivity. A significant portion of revenue in the Lending
   Technology segment is tied to loan origination volume, which historically
   declines when interest rates rise. A sustained high-rate environment could
   reduce segment revenue by an estimated 10-15%.

4. Client concentration. The Company's top ten clients accounted for
   approximately 34% of total revenue in the most recent fiscal year. The
   loss of one or more of these clients could have a material adverse effect
   on revenue.

5. Competition. The Company competes with both large incumbent financial
   software vendors and well-funded fintech startups. Increased competition
   could pressure pricing and margins.
